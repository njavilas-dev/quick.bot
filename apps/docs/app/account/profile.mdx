## Profile Information

Your account profile contains your basic information that's displayed across the QuickBot platform.

### Update Your Name

You can update your display name at any time. This name will be shown in:

- Workspace member lists
- Shared bot collaborations
- Account notifications

To update your name:

1. Click on the **Name** field.
2. Type your new display name.
3. Changes are saved automatically.

### Email Address

Your email address is used for:

- Account authentication
- Important notifications
- Password reset requests
- Workspace invitations

To change your email address:

1. Edit the **Email** field in your profile.
2. Changes will be saved automatically.

<Note>
  If you update your email, make sure it's an active address you can access, since all
  account-related emails will be sent there.
</Note>

### Profile Picture

Upload a custom profile picture to personalize your account:

1. Click the **Update Photo** button next to your avatar.
2. Select an image file from your device.
3. The image will be uploaded and applied instantly.

**Supported formats:** PNG, JPG, JPEG, GIF

## Password Management

To change your password:

1. Click the **Manage Password** button.
2. Follow the on-screen instructions to request a password reset.
3. An email will be sent to your registered address with reset instructions.

<Note>
  If you don't receive the reset email within a few minutes, check your spam folder or try again.
</Note>

## Profile Privacy

Your profile information visibility:

- **Name**: Visible to workspace members and collaborators.
- **Email**: Visible to workspace administrators only.
- **Profile Picture**: Visible to workspace members and collaborators.

QuickBot never shares your personal information with third parties without your explicit consent.
