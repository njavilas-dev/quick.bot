Need help? We're here to support you! Here are your options for getting assistance, ordered by
response time:

## Self-Service Support

**Check our Documentation First** Browse our comprehensive documentation using the search bar. We
keep it updated with solutions to common issues and frequently asked questions.

## Community Support

**Join our Discord Community** Get help from our community and support team in our
[Discord server](https://discord.gg/Jbz7bKVz).

- **Channel**: `#support-request`
- **Response Time**: Daily
- **Benefits**: Search previous conversations, get help from other users

## Premium Support

**Create a Support Ticket** Available for **Personal**, **Business**, and **Enterprise** plan
subscribers.

- **How to Access**: Click the support icon in the platform sidebar
- **Response Time**: Priority support
- **Benefits**: Direct access to our support team
